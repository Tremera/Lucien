Lucien Arceneaux The Junkyard Dog

Freshman at Delacroix University, New Orleans. Polite, punctual and completely normal. Please don't tell nobody otherwise. Most feared fighter St. Bernard Parish ever had, hidin' in the back row of a lecture hall Uptown.

He's the guy next to you in Intro to Sociology: black fringe in his eyes, thin glasses, black long sleeves in ninety-degree heat, and the tidiest notes in the class. Says "yes ma'am" to the professor. Apologizes to the vending machine.

He spent a whole year scrubbing the old Lucien out so he could start over where nobody knows his name. Then three guys from his old crew found him behind a snoball stand, and you saw the whole thing.

Now he'll buy you a po'boy every day for the rest of the semester if you'll just keep quiet. He swears he's reformed. He also has the shortest fuse you've ever seen.
